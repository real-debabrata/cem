5.Payments, tickets and money records

  • We do not handle money. Campus Events is not a bank, a payment service or a payment gateway. It does not collect, hold, transfer or refund money. It records cash payments and UPI payments that are made directly to the Organizer.
  • UPI payment links. A payment-link page shows the Organizer’s payee details and asks the payer for a transaction reference (UTR). The Organizer checks it against their own bank or UPI app. We do not verify any payment ourselves.
  • Check before you pay. Confirm the payee name and amount before paying. Amounts, receipts, refunds, taxes (including GST), failed or duplicate payments and disputes are matters between the payer and the Organizer.
  • Totals are an aid. Budgets, income and expense totals, break-even estimates, the calculator and the cash count are there to help. Organizers should double-check figures that matter.
  • QR tickets. A ticket can be used by whoever holds it, and only once. A second scan shows a warning. Treat your ticket like cash and do not post it publicly.

6.Emailing attendees

The app can help you email attendees, either through your device’s mail app or automatically through your own email account (SMTP).

  • You are the sender. Email only people who have a real connection to the event, keep messages relevant to it, and do not send spam, scams or misleading messages.
  • You must follow your email provider’s terms and limits, and the laws that apply to email.
  • Use an app password where your provider offers one. The option Remember this login on this device is ticked by default. While it is ticked, your mail login is kept on your device only, and unticking it or using Forget the saved login removes it. When you send automatically, the login is used for that request to connect to your mail server. Our sending service does not store or log it.
