1.Who we are

Campus Events (“CEM”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) is an event-management app and website for campus teams, made and run by Debabrata Das. This policy explains what personal data the app handles, why, who can see it, and what choices you have. It covers the website at cem.deba.indevs.in, the Android app, and the registration, payment and ticket pages that Organizers share with attendees.

“Personal data” means any information about a person who can be identified from it. Please read this policy together with our Terms & Conditions.

2.Who decides what: us and Organizers

Two different people are involved whenever personal data is in the app:

  • Us. We run the app, the sign-in system and the services behind it. We decide how that works and how it is kept safe.
  • Organizers and event teams. They decide which details to collect from attendees, applicants, sponsors and teammates, and why. The Terms (section 4) require them to collect only what the event needs, use it only for the event, and keep it safe.

If your details were collected for an event (for example you registered for one), the Organizer of that event is the first person to ask about how they use them. You can also write to us and we will help.

3.What we collect

Your account

  • Your name and email address, and a unique account ID.
  • If you sign in with email, your password is kept by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores only a scrambled version of it. When you first create the account, the password passes once through our sign-up service so it can create the account. We do not store or log it, and after that we cannot read it.
  • If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with the app. We do not ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Events you create or join

  • Event details, members and their roles, tasks, notes, budgets, income and expenses, bills, sponsors and vendors, attendee lists, check-in records and event chat messages.
  • Attendee lists can include names, College IDs, email addresses, phone numbers and the answers to an Organizer’s own questions.

Registrations and payments (no account needed)

  • When someone applies through a public registration page, we store what they enter: name, email address, optional College ID and phone number, answers to the Organizer’s questions, how they plan to pay, and any payment reference (UTR) they give.
  • When someone uses a payment link, we store their name, the amount and the UTR they enter.
  • We do not take or hold the money. UPI payments go straight from the payer to the Organizer.

Tickets and the camera

  • A ticket link carries the ticket details (such as name, event and date) in the part of the web address after the “#”. Browsers do not send that part to a server, so the ticket is drawn on the person’s own device.
  • The camera is used only to scan QR tickets. The scan is read on your device. We do not record, keep or upload camera images.

Sign-up codes

  • To confirm a new email account we send a 6-digit code to that address. We keep the email address and a one-way scrambled version of the code (not the code itself) for a short time, plus a count of requests from your network (IP) address, to stop abuse.

Emails you send through the app

  • If you email attendees automatically, your message, the recipients and your mail login pass through our sending service for that one request, and are used to connect to your mail provider and send. Our service does not store or log them. We keep only a daily count of messages sent, to respect limits.
  • The app offers “Remember this login on this device”, which is ticked by default. If it stays ticked, your mail login is saved in this device’s own storage. Untick it, or use Forget the saved login, to remove it.

The AI helper (optional)

  • The AI helper is off until you read the notice and turn it on, and you can turn it off again under Privacy inside the helper. It uses Google’s Gemini service on its free plan, reached through our sending service.
  • What is sent: the message you type, the event’s title, type, date and venue, the roles (not names) of team members, task group names and registration question labels. Before sending, the app replaces team member names with codes like P1, and replaces email addresses, phone numbers, UPI IDs, ID numbers and web links with placeholders. Our service removes them a second time. A name that is not on your team list cannot be recognised, so please do not type attendee names.
  • What is never sent: attendee lists and registrations, payment records, UPI IDs and payee names (these are typed on your device only), budgets, tickets and event chat.
  • On Google’s free plan, Google may use what is sent to improve its products, and people may review it. Do not type private details.
  • Our service does not store or log your messages or the AI’s answers. We keep only a daily count per signed-in user, under a scrambled ID, to respect limits. The conversation stays in memory on your device and is erased when you close the app.
  • The AI only proposes changes. Nothing is saved until you tap Confirm, and your role is checked again at that moment.

Technical information

  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the services we use (Google, Cloudflare) may record standard technical details such as IP address, browser or device type and time of access. This is normal for running a service. We do not use it to track you.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts or your photos.

4.How we use it

  • To let you sign in and to run events: roles, tasks, budgets, chat, registrations, tickets, check-in, payment records and exports.
  • To send sign-up codes and, when you ask, to send emails to attendees.
  • To keep the service safe, for example by limiting sign-up codes and emails and blocking misuse.
  • To answer your questions and requests, and to meet legal duties.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build profiles of you, and we do not sell it.

Google user data. We use the name and email address Google gives us only to sign you in and to show your name in the app. We do not sell it, use it for ads, or share it except as this policy says. Campus Events’ use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5.Who can see your information

People in your events. What a team member can see depends on their role. Every member of an event can read its attendee list, event chat is visible to its members (including people who join later), and money records are limited to roles with the Money permission.

6.Where your data is stored

Google, Cloudflare, Brevo and GitHub run data centres in many countries, so your data may be processed outside India. We rely on these providers’ own security and legal safeguards. If you do not want that, please do not use the app.

7.How long we keep it

  • Accounts and event data stay until an Organizer deletes the event, or until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Sign-up codes and request counts are short-lived. Codes expire after 10 minutes, and the records expire on their own within about an hour. The daily email count expires within two days.
  • On your device, data stays until you sign out, clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.
  • Provider backups and logs may hold copies for a short time after deletion.
  • Organizers decide how long to keep attendee details for their event, and should delete them when they are no longer needed.

8.Your rights and choices

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and in general, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data of yours we hold and how it is used;
  • correct or complete it (you can change your name yourself in Account, alerts & join event);
  • delete it, and withdraw consent you have given;
  • pass on a grievance, or name someone to exercise these rights for you if you cannot.

To make a request, email cem@deba.indevs.in. To protect your account, we may ask you to write from the email address that is on it. We aim to reply within 30 days.

What deletion means. We will delete your sign-in account and the personal details in your profile. Things you added to an event that belongs to other people, such as chat messages or check-in records, may stay in that event. Tell us if you want those removed or made anonymous, and we will look at each request. If you ask an Organizer to remove your registration details, the Organizer can delete them from their event.

You can also leave an event, and use Export data to keep a copy of an event’s records. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may have the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Act.

9.Security

  • Connections use HTTPS.
  • Database rules decide who can read or change each event’s data, based on membership and role.
  • Sign-up codes are stored only as a one-way scramble, and mail passwords are not stored by our sending service.
  • Passwords are kept by Google Firebase Authentication as a scrambled version, and our sign-up service does not store or log them.

No system is completely secure. Please keep your password and your device safe, and use an app password for your mail account. If a data breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

10.Children

The app is not meant for children. As our Terms say, you must be 18 or older, or use the app with a parent’s or guardian’s knowledge and permission. If we find that we hold a child’s data without a parent’s permission, we will delete it. A parent who thinks that has happened can write to us.

11.Cookies and storage on your device

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The app keeps some information in your browser’s or app’s own storage so it works: your sign-in, an offline copy of your events, your theme and settings, notes, calculator values, and, if you leave the option ticked, your saved mail login. Google’s sign-in screen may use its own cookies. You can clear this storage from your browser or phone settings, or by signing out.

12.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page with its date at the top. If a change is significant, we will try to tell you in the app.
