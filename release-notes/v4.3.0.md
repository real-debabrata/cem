- New: **profile pictures.** Open the profile circle (top right) > **Profile photo**, or **Account, alerts & join event** > **Change photo**. Pick a photo, drag it into place, zoom, Save
- Change or remove it any time. The new photo shows for your whole team within moments
- Your photo shows in the top bar, the side panel, the People list, the team row on Details, the Events list (a few faces on each event) and next to your messages in Chat. Anyone without a photo keeps the coloured letter
- Built to be light on Firebase: the photo is cropped and shrunk on your phone to 64 x 64 (about 3 KB), so it needs no Storage and no paid plan. Showing photos adds no database reads, and chat messages stay the same size
- **Before updating the app, publish the new `firestore.rules`** (Firebase console > Firestore Database > Rules). It lets a member store a small photo next to their name, and nothing else
- Privacy Policy updated with a line about profile photos
- No Worker change. **Update normally:** Profile menu > **Update app** (Android) or just reload the website
