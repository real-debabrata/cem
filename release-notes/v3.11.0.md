- New: **designed ticket pictures**. Every ticket is now a proper ticket PNG (name, event, date, time, venue, QR) instead of a bare QR code. Two looks: **Gradient** (purple, QR on top, tear-off stub) and **Flat** (white, striped edges, QR at the bottom)
- **One QR code only, no barcode.** The QR is exactly the one the scanner already reads, so check-in, offline scanning and the one-use rule are unchanged
- **Email tickets (Automatic/SMTP)**: each email now shows the attendee's own ticket picture and attaches it as a PNG they can save. **My mail app** mode sends the ticket link, which opens the same picture
- **Save for offline use**: the ticket page has **Save ticket (PNG)** and **Share or save to Photos**, remembers the ticket on the phone, and keeps its own files so the link opens even without internet
- Organizers: **Attendees > ... > Ticket** shows the picture with a style switch, **Save ticket (PNG)**, **Share image**, link, WhatsApp and email. **Email tickets** has a style switch and a live preview
- Older ticket links keep working. If your Cloudflare Worker is still on 3.10.x, emails go out with the small QR as before and the app tells you to update it (one paste, see SETUP.md, *Upgrade to 3.11.0*)
