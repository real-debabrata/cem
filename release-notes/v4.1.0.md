- New: **AI helper.** Open an event and tap **✨ AI**. Type what you need in plain words and it drafts an event description, a payment link, registration questions, a task (with person and due date) or an item to buy. It shows a card you can edit. Nothing is saved until you tap **Confirm**, and your role is checked again at that moment
- **Off until you turn it on.** The first time, a short notice explains what is shared and you tick a box to continue. **Privacy** inside the helper lets you turn it off and forget your choice
- **Names and numbers are hidden before anything leaves your phone.** Team names become codes (P1, P2). Emails, phone numbers, UPI IDs, ID numbers and links are replaced. The Worker checks again
- **Never sent:** attendee lists, registrations, payments, budgets, tickets and chat. Payee name and UPI ID are typed on your phone only
- The server keeps no message text, only a daily count per person (40 messages by default). The chat is erased when you close the app
- It uses Google Gemini on the free plan, so Google may use what you type to improve its products. Do not type private details or attendee names
- Needs you to be signed in and online. The **Privacy Policy** has a new "AI helper" section
- **Admins:** update the Worker, add one secret `GEMINI_API_KEY`, and add `ai.js`. Steps are in `AI-SETUP.md`. No Firebase rules change
