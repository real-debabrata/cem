- Fix: **the public registration form no longer forces a College ID.** Outsiders and guests without one can now register. Only the **email address** is always required
- **Everything else is up to the organizer and the event.** College ID, phone and every other answer appear and are required only if the event's own form asks for them (the questions and their **Required** tick). If the event has no phone question, a plain **Phone / WhatsApp** box is still shown, and it is optional
- **One application per email address** (it was one per College ID). A person who applies twice with the same email is refused. When you approve, the app also checks whether that College ID or email is already an attendee and links them instead of adding them twice
- Applications without a College ID no longer show a blank "College ID" in **Applications to review**, and attendees made from them simply have no College ID. Ticket scanning, email merge and CSV export already coped with that
- **Needs one change in Firebase: publish the new `firestore.rules`.** Until you do, the public form is refused. Applications already received (filed under a College ID) keep working and can still be approved
- Existing events are not changed: if a form already has a College ID question ticked **Required**, it stays required until you untick it in the event's form
