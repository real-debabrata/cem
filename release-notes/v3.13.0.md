- New: **a cleaner Events tab.** Events now sit in one tidy list, one row per event, instead of a grid of boxes. Each row shows the date, name, type, time, venue and how many people have registered, with a slim progress bar
- **Dropdowns instead of buttons.** Pick a **Type** and a **Status** (All, Upcoming or Past) from dropdowns that show how many events each choice holds. A dropdown that is filtering turns indigo, so you can see at a glance that the list is narrowed. A new **Sort** menu orders by **Upcoming first**, **Latest first**, **Name A–Z** or **Most registered**
- **Search that keeps up.** It now finds events by name, venue or type, updates as you type without losing your place, and has a clear (×) button. **Clear filters** appears whenever something is filtering, next to a count such as "2 of 8 events"
- **Tap the whole row to open an event.** The separate **Manage** button is gone
- **Easier to scan:** today's event is highlighted, tomorrow reads "Tomorrow", events that are over are muted and marked "Ended", and an event with no seats left shows "Full" in amber. Upcoming and past events get their own headings when you have both. Meeting links show just the site name
- Friendlier empty screens: a first-run message with a **New event** button, and a "No matching events" message with **Clear filters**
- Works with the keyboard and screen readers, on phones (the dropdowns open your phone's own picker), and in light and dark mode
- **Files only.** No Firebase rules change, no Worker change, no new variable or secret. The Dashboard and the event pages are unchanged
