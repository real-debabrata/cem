- New: **Email attendees in three simple steps.** The email window is now a guided flow: **1 People**, **2 Message**, **3 Send**. Built so someone who has never heard of SMTP can use it
- **People:** tabs for **Not emailed**, **Emailed** and **No email**, so you can see exactly who has and has not been emailed (with the date, and whether they got a ticket or an invitation). Add a missing email address right there
- **Not every event needs a ticket.** A tick box, **Send a ticket with a QR code**, chooses between a ticket email and a plain **invitation**. Untick it and there is no QR, no ticket picture and no ticket link. The standard subject and message switch to an invitation, and your own wording is never overwritten. Remembered per event on your device
- **Send:** pick **This device's mail app** or **My email account (automatic)**. For the second, tap your provider (**Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, Zoho Mail (India or World), Brevo, Other**, each with its logo and name) and enter your email address and app password. The server and port are filled in; **Server settings** is there if you need it
- **Help right where you need it:** a **How do I get this password?** button shows the steps for your provider, with a button straight to the Google App passwords page or Brevo's SMTP page
- **Buttons, not text links:** every action in the window (Mark all as emailed, Clear emailed marks, Reset message, Show password, Forget the saved login, Download CSV and more) is now a real button. The attendee list button is renamed **Email attendees**
- Works on phones (the window slides up from the bottom, with Back and Next always in view), in light and dark mode
- **No Worker change.** The 3.11.0 Worker already handles an email without a ticket picture; no new variable, secret, Firestore rule or Android plugin. Old "emailed" marks keep working
- Gmail, Zoho and Brevo logos are from the open `simple-icons` set (CC0); Yahoo and Outlook are simple badges. All names and logos belong to their owners
